# Fennec trademark and brand policy

Fennec's source code and brand asset files are available under the
[MIT License](LICENSE). That copyright license does not grant permission to use
Fennec's visual identity as a trademark or to suggest that another product,
service, or organization is an official Fennec offering.

This policy describes the permission Ryan Fitzpatrick grants for use of the
original Fennec visual identity. It does not limit rights granted by the MIT
License or rights available under applicable trademark law, such as fair or
nominative use.

## Covered marks

The covered marks are Fennec's original visual brand features:

- the F-first monogram with its abstract fox-ear silhouette;
- the horizontal lockups combining that monogram with the Fennec wordmark; and
- the web, application, installer, and tray icons derived from the monogram.

Together, this policy calls them the **Fennec Marks**. The standalone word
"Fennec" is not asserted here as exclusively owned. Rocket League, its vehicle
names, and all related marks belong to their respective owners.

## Uses that do not require permission

You may, without asking:

- truthfully refer to the Fennec project, link to its official repository or
  website, or say that another project is compatible with or based on Fennec;
- show screenshots in which a Fennec Mark appears as part of the unmodified
  application or its documentation;
- keep the Fennec Marks in a source-code fork used to study, test, or contribute
  changes to the official project; and
- redistribute an exact, unmodified official release with its marks intact if
  you identify the official source and do not imply that you operate or are
  endorsed by Fennec.

These permissions do not allow you to use a Fennec Mark as the brand for your
own product, service, organization, or community.

## Modified versions and hosted forks

Publicly distributed modified builds and third-party hosted forks must remove
the Fennec Marks and use an independent visual identity. They may truthfully
describe themselves as "based on Fennec" as long as the presentation does not
suggest sponsorship, endorsement, or official status.

Rebranding does not change the MIT License requirements. Copies or substantial
portions of the software must still include its copyright and permission
notices.

## Uses that require permission

Written permission is required to:

- alter, recolor, animate, combine, or create a derivative of a Fennec Mark;
- use a Fennec Mark as a standalone logo outside the uses allowed above;
- place a Fennec Mark on merchandise or promotional goods; or
- use a Fennec Mark in a product listing, domain name, social-media identity,
  event identity, or organization name.

Open a request in the
[Fennec issue tracker](https://github.com/ryanf9802/Fennec/issues) describing the
proposed use. Permission must be granted in writing before the use begins.

## No third-party endorsement

Fennec is an independent community project. This policy grants no rights in
marks owned by Epic Games, Psyonix, or any other third party, and it does not
suggest that those parties sponsor or endorse Fennec.
