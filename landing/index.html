More than a scoreboard

The story of how you actually played.

Rank tells you where you landed. Fennec keeps the useful context from the games that got you there.

01

Every game, live and saved

Follow the score, teams, events, touches, demos, passes, and 50s during a match. Revisit the same detail afterward.

02

Sessions with context

See records, streaks, trends, recurring teammates, and player encounters across a stretch of play—not just isolated results.

03

Explainable ball analytics

Explore touch maps, hit speed, possession context, scoring touches, saves, and challenges derived from observed events.

Built to stay yours

Your data lives on your machine.

Match history is stored in your browser. Fennec has no account system and no hosted database of your games. Export a complete backup whenever you want one.

The browser app is the complete experience. An optional Windows companion can automate capture and keep collecting while the browser is closed.
