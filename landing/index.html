Analytics Rocket League leaves out

See the plays behind the scoreboard.

Fennec turns observed ball events into consistent, inspectable stats. Passes, challenges, and field position become part of your match history instead of disappearing at the final whistle.

01

Passes

Fennec credits a pass when the next identifiable single-player touch comes from a different teammate during the same active play.

An opponent touch, ambiguous multi-player hit, or dead-ball transition breaks the sequence.

02

50s

Fennec credits each identified player when opponents contact the ball on the same hit or within 250 milliseconds of one another.

Rapid follow-up contacts within half a second remain one challenge instead of inflating the count.

03

3D touch map

Map every touch, 50, save, and goal in a rotatable 3D arena, then filter the action by player or team.

Also included: team touch share, post-hit speed, speed gained or lost, last-touch control, session trends, and recurring teammates.

Built to stay yours

Your data lives on your machine.

Match history is stored in your browser. Fennec has no account system and no hosted database of your games. Export a complete backup whenever you want one.

The browser app is the complete experience. An optional Windows companion can automate capture and keep collecting while the browser is closed.
